# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Crossfade between tracks (off / 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 12 s), preloads the next track
- Fullscreen visualizer
- Three new visualizers: radial, spectrogram, particles (six in total)
- Richer home screen: pulsing monochrome icon, recent and most played tracks, totals
- Listening stats screen (7-day chart, top tracks, top artists, recent)

## 1.0.0
- Black terminal-style local music player (Electron, Windows)
- Pick a music folder once, playlist with shuffle and repeat
- Album covers and full track details from ID3 tags
- Bars, waveform and matrix visualizers
- Keyboard-only controls
